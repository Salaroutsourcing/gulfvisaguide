Gulf Salary Guide 2026

Direct answer: Average monthly salaries in the Gulf vary by country, profession and experience. Many packages are tax-free and often include housing, transport or annual tickets. Below are typical ranges for mid-level professionals based on current market observations.

United Arab Emirates (AED)

  • Civil / Mechanical Engineer: 12,000 – 22,000
  • Registered Nurse: 8,000 – 14,000
  • Software Developer: 12,000 – 25,000
  • Hotel Manager: 10,000 – 18,000
  • Accountant: 8,000 – 15,000

Saudi Arabia (SAR)

  • Civil / Mechanical Engineer: 10,000 – 20,000
  • Registered Nurse: 8,000 – 14,000
  • Software Developer: 12,000 – 22,000
  • Accountant: 7,000 – 14,000

Qatar (QAR)

  • Civil / Mechanical Engineer: 12,000 – 22,000
  • Registered Nurse: 9,000 – 15,000
  • Software Developer: 14,000 – 25,000

Important Notes

These figures are approximate mid-range values and can be higher for senior roles or lower for junior positions. Always evaluate the full package (housing, flights, insurance, end-of-service benefits) rather than basic salary alone. Cost of living differs significantly between cities.
