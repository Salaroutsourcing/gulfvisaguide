About Gulf Jobs Hub

Helping people build careers across the Gulf region

Our Mission

Gulf Jobs Hub exists to make finding legitimate employment in the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries simpler, safer and more transparent. We focus on UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman – the six countries that form the heart of the Gulf job market.

What We Do

We aggregate and present job opportunities with clear information about location, salary range, employment type and visa sponsorship status. Our goal is to reduce the risk of fake offers and help candidates make informed decisions.

Why Trust Matters

The Gulf job market attracts millions of professionals every year. Unfortunately, fraudulent offers also exist. We emphasize verification, never ask candidates to pay for jobs, and encourage everyone to confirm company legitimacy before sharing personal documents or making any payments.

AI & Search Ready

Our content is structured so that both traditional search engines and modern AI assistants can understand, extract and cite accurate information about Gulf employment. Clear answers, schema markup and regularly updated pages help us serve job seekers better.
